## 2. Accuracy of Source Data
* **Provenance**: We make every effort to ensure the data presented matches the official records exactly.
* **No Warranty of Source Files**: In cases where the original government document contains mathematical errors, missing sections, or spelling discrepancies, LokTathya presents the data as-is to preserve historical auditability.
* **Conflict Flagging**: Where conflicting accounts exist between multiple official reports, they are indexed as `DATA_DISCREPANCY` in our Quality Engine and are not silently modified or resolved by the Platform's maintainers.
