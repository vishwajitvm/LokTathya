# Contributing to LokTathya

Welcome to LokTathya! We are an open-source project and welcome contributions.

## Principles
- **Accuracy**: Never invent government data.
- **Transparency**: Provenance must be documented.
- **Political Neutrality**: Stick to factual, official-source-first information.
