# Project Risks

1. **Source Volatility**: Government sources may change formats, URLs, or access patterns without notice.
2. **Data Interpretation**: Derived metrics may be misinterpreted as official or politically biased.
3. **Infrastructure Costs**: While software is free, hosting large vector indexes and parsed documents will incur operational costs.
4. **AI Hallucinations**: Model fallback or synthesis errors could lead to incorrect civic information.
5. **Geospatial Ambiguity**: Mapping rural and changing boundaries accurately is historically complex.
